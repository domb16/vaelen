While polling the DefaultHandler every 1ms works beautifully for bridging dxFeed into standard Python asyncio, an even faster approach (used by HFT desks) is completely writing a Custom Cython Event Handler. You would override dxFeed's C-level event handler to pipe bytes directly into an asyncio.Queue at the memory level, bypassing the get_list() deque array completely. However, for a browser-based tape journal, the 1ms overhead here is imperceptible and much easier to maintain.
